# hard sci-fi salvage thriller — Juno Arak

## Setting

The year is 2291. The player is Juno Arak, a salvage pilot who works the debris belt around the gas giant Tessaly with her small tug, the Kestrel.
Her mentor, Captain Odile Marsh, vanished three weeks ago while surveying the derelict research station Meridian-9. Odile's last transmission was a single word: "Lantern".
Key figures: Priya Sen (Kestrel's engineer, sarcastic and loyal), Brother Calix (leader of a scavenger cult living in the belt), Director Hal Voren (head of the Ostrander Mining Concern, which wants Meridian-9 sealed), Mox (a damaged maintenance robot that follows Juno), and Dr. Ilse Brandt (a scientist who survived on Meridian-9).
Key places: the Kestrel, Meridian-9's docking ring, the hydroponics deck, the Ostrander refinery, the cult's shrine-ship Vigil, Port Halcyon (a crowded trade hub), the Tessaly cloud skimmer platform.
Juno carries a plasma cutter, a cracked data slate with Odile's survey logs, and a brass compass that belonged to Odile.
Grounded, tense, no magic; physics and air supply matter.

## Opening

A sharp hiss cuts through the Kestrel’s cabin, and the pressure gauge drops one notch. You clamp a glove over the seam in your suit while Tessaly’s blue clouds roll beyond the cracked canopy. Ahead, Meridian-9 turns slowly among tumbling scrap, its docking ring dark except for one amber lamp blinking through the dust.

“Tell me that’s not our air leaving,” Priya says over the intercom.

Mox bumps against your boot, its one working eye fixed on the station. Your cracked slate wakes in your hand. Odile’s last survey log flickers across the screen—and beneath it, a fresh transmission resolves into one word:

LANTERN.

The tug’s docking arm catches on a twisted strut. Metal groans. Your oxygen timer begins counting down, and the station’s outer hatch starts to drift open, exposing a black corridor beyond. You can cut the jammed strut before the Kestrel is pulled against the ring, or abandon the docking attempt while you still have thrust.

## Chapter 1: the Kestrel, approaching Meridian-9
